Schengen Regulatory Framework

Understanding Your Schengen Visit Visa

A clear, objective breakdown of European consular rules to prepare your application accurately.

What is a Schengen Short-Stay Visa?

The short-stay Uniform Schengen Visa (Type C) allows qualifying travelers to travel throughout the 29 member nations of the European Schengen Area for tourism, visiting relatives or friends, or attending business events for up to 90 days within any 180-day period.

90/180 Day Rule: Calculated on a rolling 180-day window across the entire Schengen Zone.

Which Embassy Should You Apply Through?

Applicants cannot choose an embassy out of personal preference or queue availability. Schengen rules mandate that your application must be lodged with the Member State that constitutes the main destination in terms of length or main purpose of your stay.

Equal Duration Rule: If visiting multiple countries for identical durations, you must apply to the country of first physical entry.

Individualized Assessment & Processing

Visa processing times and documentation requirements can differ substantially based on applicant circumstances, nationality, employment status, and the diplomatic mission in Sri Lanka. Early preparation is essential.

Official Sources: Always verify requirements with the official consular authority or accredited visa center.

A Schengen short-stay visa (Type C) allows eligible travelers to enter and travel freely within the 29 countries of the Schengen Area for up to 90 days within any 180-day period. It is designed for leisure tourism, visiting relatives or friends, business meetings, and short-stay educational or cultural events. It does not permit employment in the Schengen Area.

Under Article 5 of the Schengen Visa Code:
• If your visit involves only one country, you must apply to that country’s consular mission.
• If visiting multiple countries, you must apply to the country that constitutes your main destination in terms of the length or purpose of stay.
• If no main destination can be determined (e.g. equal duration in multiple states), you must apply through the country whose external border you will cross first. You cannot choose an embassy based on convenience or perceived ease.

Standard requirements include a current passport valid for at least 3 months after departure, two biometric photos, a duly completed Schengen application form, travel medical insurance with €30,000 coverage, round-trip flight itineraries, confirmed accommodation, bank statements proving financial solvency, and proof of employment, business ownership, or studies in Sri Lanka. Documentation criteria vary by consular mission, and customers are guided to the official consular source for their specific submission.

You can apply up to 6 months before your intended departure date (up to 9 months for seafarers), and no later than 15 calendar days before travel.
